The Mappa della Fioritura Reading for Children was created to give you a clear and empathetic compass. 🌿 Age 6 - 12 The Growing Root

🧭 Age 12 - 20 The Horizons of the Future

An in-depth analysis designed to support teenagers in the search for their own identity, in school/university orientation and to improve communication within the family during this transition.
