My name is Silvia and my professional story began with a degree in Education Sciences. For many years I worked closely with families and children, but it was with the birth of my daughter that something inside me went through a true blossoming.

From this personal transformation came Mappa della Fioritura ("The Blossoming Map"): a tool of love and discovery that uses the Destiny Matrix and date of birth to help adults and young people deeply understand their own identity, their talents, and their relationship dynamics.
